About Us

StockPilot is a simple inventory manager for small shops, stores and workshops. It replaces notebooks and scattered spreadsheets with one clear place for everything you stock.

We built it around one question: "How many do I have right now?" Every purchase and sale updates your stock automatically, so the answer is always current.

Everything runs in your browser and your data stays on your device. Prices and totals are shown in Indian Rupees (₹).

  • Shops and storesKnow what to reorder before shelves go empty.
  • Warehouses and workshopsTrack who supplies what, and what came in or went out.
  • Owners and managersSee stock value and movement in one report.

Features

Everything you need to keep stock under control, and nothing you have to set up.

Live dashboard

Totals, low-stock items and recent movements on one screen.

Product management

Add, edit and search products with automatic IDs and category filters.

Stock In / Out

Record every movement. Quantities update by themselves and can never go negative.

Low-stock alerts

Set a minimum for each product and get warned as soon as stock drops below it.

Categories and suppliers

Group products and keep supplier contacts next to the items they provide.

Reports and CSV export

See stock value in ₹, filter by date and download a CSV for Excel.

How It Works

Four steps from an empty list to a stock you can trust.

  1. Add your products

    Enter each item with its category, supplier, price and minimum stock level.

  2. Record stock in and out

    Log purchases and sales. StockPilot updates the quantity right away.

  3. Watch the alerts

    Items that fall below their minimum appear on your dashboard so you can reorder.

  4. Review reports

    Check stock value, filter by date and export a CSV when you need one.
